For organizations

Put this information on your own website.

The embed delivers current legislation and semantic HTML. It does not deliver a stylesheet. Your website remains in control of fonts, colors, spacing, borders, and layout.

One source

Maintain the legislative information centrally rather than asking every organization to research and rewrite the same bill.

Your design

The embed inherits the visual system of the host website instead of arriving as a foreign-looking widget.

Official data

Congress.gov data can be retrieved server-side while plain-English editorial explanations remain clearly identified.

A complete embed can be this small:

<div data-osw-legislation data-bill="119-s-419"></div>
<script src="https://YOUR-SITE.netlify.app/embed.js" defer></script>
